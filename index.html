Happy Birthday, meri Manuu. ♡ Pata hai baby, mujhe tumhara birthday bahut pasand hai. Shayad tumse bhi zyada excitement mujhe hoti hai is din ki. Lekin mujhe ye bhi pata hai ki tumhare liye ye din utna special ya khushi wala nahi hota. Mujhe nahi pata ki is din ke saath tumhari kitni saari buri memories judi hui hain, ya kyun tumhe lagta hai ki tumhare birthday par hamesha kuch na kuch galat ho jaata hai. Main shayad tumhari har feeling ko poori tarah samajh na paaun, lekin main kabhi nahi chahta ki tumhe apni feelings chhupani padein. Baby, aaj tumhe zabardasti khush hone ki zarurat nahi hai. Na hi kisi ke liye smile karne ka pressure lena hai. Agar mann khush hai toh dil kholkar hasna, aur agar mann udaas hai toh woh bhi bilkul okay hai. Bas meri ek chhoti si wish hai ki iss baar tumhara ye din thoda alag ho. Thoda peaceful, thoda beautiful aur bahut saare pyaar se bhara hua. Koi pareshani tumhari smile na chheen paaye, koi chhoti si baat tumhara mood kharab na kare aur tumhe ek baar bhi aisa feel na ho ki tum akeli ho. Tum sirf apne birthday par hi nahi, har ek din special ho mere liye. Tumhari hasi, tumhari baatein, tumhara gussa, tumhari bachkani harkatein aur tumhara woh caring nature — mujhe tumhara har version pasand hai. Mujhe birthdays pasand hain, aur tumhara birthday toh sabse zyada. Lekin birthday se bhi zyada mujhe tum pasand ho. Happy Birthday, meri favourite person. I hope iss saal zindagi tumhare saath thodi zyada gentle rahe.♡

If you ever forget how special you are, come back here. I'll leave this little corner of the internet right where you can find it.

18 November · For my Manuu

Meri Manuu, ♡

Meri pyaari Manuu, ♡ Sabse pehle toh Happy Birthday, meri jaan! Pata hai, main kaafi time se tumhare birthday ka wait kar raha tha. Mere liye ye din bahut special hai, kyunki aaj hi ke din meri favourite ladki is duniya mein aayi thi. Lekin ek baat hai jo main tumse kehna chahta hoon. Mujhe pata hai tumhe apna birthday bilkul pasand nahi. Shayad is din se judi kuch aisi memories hain jo tumhe khush hone nahi deti. Shayad tumhare mann mein pehle se hi ek ajeeb sa darr rehta hai ki pata nahi aaj kya ho jayega, kya galat ho jayega. Aur sach kahun toh mujhe ye sochkar bura lagta hai ki jis din tumhe sabse zyada pyaar aur happiness feel honi chahiye, usi din ko lekar tumhare mann mein itni negativity hai. Kaash main tumhari saari buri memories ko mita pata. Kaash main guarantee de pata ki aaj kuch bhi galat nahi hoga. Lekin main aisa koi jhootha promise nahi karunga. Main bas itna chahta hoon ki tum mere saath kabhi pretend mat karna. Khush nahi ho toh khush hone ka natak mat karna. Kuch bura lage toh mujhe batana. Mann na ho toh chup rehna. Tumhe mere saamne har waqt perfect ya happy banne ki zarurat nahi hai. Aur ek baat, mujhe yaad hai humari shuruaat kaise hui thi. June 2025 ka woh random Instagram group chat, tumhara admin hona, dheere-dheere follow karna, stories par mentions, DMs aur phir na jaane kab tum meri life ka itna important part ban gayi. Beech mein kuch chapters easy nahi the. Kuch dooriyan aayi, kuch misunderstandings hui, lekin 17 September 2026 ko jab hum dobara ek saath aaye aur apni relationship shuru ki, toh mere liye woh din bhi bahut special ban gaya. Aur aaj main bas thankful hoon ki tum meri life mein ho. Manuu, main tumhare saath sirf birthdays celebrate nahi karna chahta. Main tumhare saath ordinary days bhi jeena chahta hoon. Tumhari random baatein sunna chahta hoon, tumhari chhoti-chhoti achievements par khush hona chahta hoon, tumhare bure din mein tumhara saath dena chahta hoon aur tumhari silly harkaton par hasna chahta hoon. Main chahta hoon ki jab kabhi tumhe lage ki sab kuch theek nahi chal raha, toh tumhe kam se kam ek insaan aisa mile jiske saamne tum bina soche apna dil rakh sako. Aur baby, ek din shayad tumhara birthday tumhe itna bura na lage. Main ye nahi chahta ki tum apni feelings badalne ke liye khud ko force karo. Bas chahta hoon ki waqt ke saath is date ke saath kuch achhi memories bhi jud jaayein. Meri wish hai ki iss saal tumhare saare chhote-bade dreams poore hon. Tumhe woh happiness mile jiske liye tum deserve karti ho. Tumhara mann halka rahe, tumhari smile bani rahe aur tum khud ko kabhi kam na samjho. Aur haan, ek baat kabhi mat bhoolna: Tumhe pyaar paane ke liye perfect hone ki zarurat nahi hai. Tumhe care deserve karne ke liye hamesha smile karne ki zarurat nahi hai. Tum jaisi ho, bilkul waisi hi mere liye bahut precious ho. I love you, meri Manuu. Shayad main har baar sahi words mein express na kar paaun, lekin tum mere liye bahut मायने rakhti ho. Happy Birthday once again, meri jaan. Aaj ka din jaisa bhi ho, tum mere liye hamesha special rahogi.

I hope this new year of your life is full of peace, courage, health, silly laughter and beautiful surprises. You deserve all of it.

Happy Birthday, meri jaan.
